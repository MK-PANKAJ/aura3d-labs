Frequently Asked Questions

What distinguishes PLA+ from standard PLA formulations?
PLA+ incorporates macromolecular impact modifiers that improve mechanical toughness up to 5x. Layer bonding is noticeably stronger, spool brittleness is mitigated, and fluid melt characteristics permit sustained speeds up to 600 mm/s on high-acceleration CoreXY setups.
Are GST tax invoices provided for institutional ITC claims?
Yes. Every order is issued with a fully compliant B2B GST tax invoice containing your official GSTIN, legal registered entity name, and HSN classifications (8477 for machinery, 3916 for filament polymers).
What is the Pan-India express dispatch timeline?
Orders finalized before 14:00 IST on business days undergo automated batch dispatch that same afternoon via priority courier partners (Delhivery Air, BlueDart). Tier-1 metro destinations typically receive delivery within 24 to 48 hours.
